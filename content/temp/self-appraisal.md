# Self-Appraisal Nicklas Chapman September 2026

### Reflecting on January to August, what has gone well and where have you made a positive contribution?

I'm happy with my performance this year.  On a BAU front, the Merchant team is functioning well, with metrics looking good and improving.  We've seen a lot of change over the past few months and maintained a steady ship through it: new joiners have embedded quickly and well, services have been handed over cleanly, and changes in the product org around the team have been embraced and handled without disruption.

The team have engaged well with the new remit and roadmap, and good collaboration between me and Product has provided a solid foundation for that adoption.

On the DevOps side, I recognised the need to overhaul our approach to our SRE On Call rota and tackled it proactively.  The timing turned out to be serendipitous as the work fed directly into the changes made to get all teams on call.  The impact was significant - not just in terms of statistics - but on quality of life for engineers in the DevOps and Platform teams.

I've encouraged increasing autonomy in both teams - Jacek has owned and run the card-tracking (IC) project for Merchant, Nick Gilbert is owning Ephemerals for DevOps, and Stefan is running the module federation work.

After a little over two years at Zilch, I continue to show up with dedication and remain fully engaged.

### Where do you see opportunities to improve, develop or enhance your impact?

The AdTech domain that Merchant is moving into presents a conspicuous chance to enhance my impact.  It has not escaped me that optimising the revenue we're able to generate through retailers and advertisers can have very significant impact on the fortunes of the company.  I look forward to being able to boast some early successes by the next full appraisal process.

In reflecting on the events of this year for opportunities to improve, Iteration 13 planning springs to mind.  Planning was a little scrappy.  This was largely due to change-over in Product.  But I was on leave for the two weeks leading up to planning day and recognise that I could have anticipated issues and delegated planning support better for my absence.

The DevOps team needs a little work.  I've identified a cultural/mindset challenge and have invested some time in coaching, but will need to pay the team more attention or find more direct approaches.

### Final Summary

I think I've had a solid year.  Both teams are delivering consistently, with no fires burning. The improvements I've made are well bedded-in, allowing me to look for further improvements rather than maintain discipline on older ones.  

CMP/AdTech is the obvious opportunity on the horizon to add value, but I'm also looking for ways to have impact that crosses team boundaries and shows broader impact within Engineering and the broader company.

I hope my track record shows I can be trusted with things that matter and that I'm transparent enough to surface things when they're not going to plan.
